What does the RANDOM MIX button do?

The RANDOM MIX button when enabled will give you a random ratio on how to mix your drink!

Where can I find the reviews of this website?

You can find the reviews under the "reviews" tab on the navigation buttons!

I accidentally filled in "Fanta" in my liquor list how can I remove that?

If you have filled in the wrong liquor or mixer you can easily remove it by pressing on the "X" button next to it.

When would I use this website?

"What To Mix" is a website that you can use at your party, when you are out of ideas or when you just want to try something new!

How do I know if I have the right ingredients?

You don't need to be scared about not having the right ingredients! Because you you fill in you liquor and your mixer and "WTM" will make you a delicious (or not!) drink!
